Settings

Height and weight are used for calories, and for estimating steps when the motion sensor isn't available. Everything is stored only on this device.

Getting accurate tracking

  • Keep this app open with the screen on. Phones stop giving web apps GPS and motion data when the screen locks or you switch apps.
  • Wait for the GPS pill to turn green before you set off.
  • Carry the phone in your hand or a pocket so it can feel each step.
  • Add it to your home screen for a full-screen app that works offline.
